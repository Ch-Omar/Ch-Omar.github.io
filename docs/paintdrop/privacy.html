PaintDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the game PaintDrop ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Ch-Omar Games ("we"). Contact: chakroun.omar91@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings, stats) is saved on your device only.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play. These Google services collect some device information, as described below.
  • We use Firebase Analytics to see, in general, which levels are too hard and where players stop, so we can improve the game.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device (level reached, coins, boosters, lives, stars, cups, Paint Pass progress, achievements, the name and avatar you choose in the profile, and your settings). This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game. The profile name is only shown inside the game.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example "Your lives are full" or "Your daily gift is ready"). They are created by the game on your device; no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings > Notifications or in your phone's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including optional videos you can watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

Purchases (Google Play)

If you buy coins, bundles or "No ads", the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase so the game can give you what you bought and restore it later.

Analytics (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous information about how the game is played (for example: levels started, won and lost, boosters used, ads watched, purchases made) together with device information such as the model, operating system version and an app instance identifier. We use it only to fix problems and to balance the levels. Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Play and Google Firebase), who process it under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Progress saved on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Analytics data kept by Firebase is retained for the period set in our Firebase settings and is then deleted. To ask us to delete any data linked to your device, email chakroun.omar91@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information and to object to its use. To use these rights, email chakroun.omar91@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
