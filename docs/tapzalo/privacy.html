Tapzalo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the app Tapzalo ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Ch-Omar Games ("we"). Contact: chakroun.omar91@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. We don't ask for your email or phone number.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, scores, coins, boosters, settings, stats) is saved on your device.
  • If you take part in the online leaderboard, your player name, avatar and scores are stored by Unity Gaming Services and shown to other players.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play. These Google services collect some device information, as described below.
  • We use Unity Analytics to see, in general, which levels are too hard and where players stop, so we can improve the games.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress and settings on your device (levels won, stars and best scores of each game, coins, boosters, daily gift streak, achievements, the name and avatar you choose in the profile, and your settings). This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Online leaderboard (Unity Gaming Services)

To rank players, the app signs in to Unity Gaming Services anonymously: Unity creates a random player identifier for your device; no email, phone number or social account is used. Your player name (the one you choose in the profile, 3 to 16 characters), your avatar and your scores are sent to Unity's leaderboard service and are visible to other players of Tapzalo. Choose a name that does not identify you if you prefer to stay anonymous. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a reminder on your device when your daily gift is ready. It is created by the app on your device; no information is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in Settings > Notifications or in your phone's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including optional videos you can watch for a reward (coins, a booster, a continue). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in the app's settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

Purchases (Google Play)

If you buy coins, the starter bundle or "Remove ads", the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase so the app can give you what you bought and restore it later.

Analytics (Unity Analytics)

We use Unity Analytics to collect information about how the app is played (for example: levels started, won and lost, boosters used, ads watched, purchases made) together with device information such as the model, operating system version and an installation identifier. We use it only to fix problems and to balance the levels. Unity's privacy information: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Play and Unity Gaming Services), who process it under their own privacy policies. Leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players.

Data retention and deletion

Progress saved on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. Leaderboard entries and analytics data are kept by Unity for the periods set in our Unity project and are then deleted. To ask us to delete your leaderboard entry or any data linked to your device, email chakroun.omar91@gmail.com with your player name.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information and to object to its use. To use these rights, email chakroun.omar91@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
